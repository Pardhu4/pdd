How SurplusToServe Works

“SurplusToServe connects food providers, donors, and volunteers with those in need, helping to reduce waste and meet essential needs. Join us in making a difference!”

Our Process

Step 1: Donors and food providers sign up and post available surplus items or donations.
Step 2: SurplusToServe verifies the donations to ensure they meet safety guidelines and quality standards.
Step 3: Volunteers are notified to pick up the items and deliver them to recipients.
Step 4: Individuals or organizations in need receive donations, including meals, clothing, and essential supplies.
Step 5: SurplusToServe tracks all contributions and ensures efficient distribution to reduce waste and support communities.
